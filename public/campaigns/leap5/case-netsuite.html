Unifying Council Finance Without Duplicating Billing, Asset or Works Data

NetSuite Financial Management as the finance core for the Shire of Irwin, integrated with iTANZ CPM and Council Asset Management so revenue, asset and works transactions reach the ledger without duplicate entry.

The Problem

  • The Shire required a corporate finance platform that could mirror municipal, reserve, trust, fund, program and cost-centre reporting while remaining integrated to the existing rating and asset/works systems.
  • Journal approvals, AP, AR, banking and reporting needed stronger workflow and automation without breaking the financial mapping already used by CPM and WAM.
  • Finance also needed Australian banking outputs and statutory reporting aligned to AASB and WA local-government requirements.

The Solution

  • iTANZ implemented NetSuite Financial Management across General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable and bank/cash management.
  • Configured class, department, location and custom-segment structures to mirror the Shire’s reporting model, with SuiteFlow approvals for journals and budget adjustments.
  • Mapped CPM rate, FSL, infringement and WAM asset transactions into the correct fund/cost-centre combinations.
  • Implemented vendor bill capture, three-way matching, approval routing, EFT/ABA payments, remittance advice and recurring/prepaid schedules.
  • Configured sundry debtor invoicing, bank feeds and statement auto-reconciliation.
  • Delivered SuiteAnalytics workbooks and dashboards for budget-versus-actual, cash-flow and BAS/GST reporting, supporting statutory financial statement preparation.
Technologies Used

Oracle NetSuite Financial Management • SuiteFlow • SuiteAnalytics • NetSuite bank feeds and reconciliation • iTANZ CPM integration • iTANZ Council Asset Management / WAM integration • EFT / ABA banking files

Impact We Created

A connected finance core

Created a finance core that receives rating, asset and works transactions without duplicate entry.

Automated finance operations

Automated key approval, payment, recurring-expense and bank-reconciliation processes.

Sundry debtors, one GL cycle

Maintained separate treatment for non-rates debtors while reconciling to the same GL cycle.

Statutory reporting in place

Gave Council finance teams operational and statutory reporting through NetSuite analytics and saved-search structures.

The Conclusion

The Shire of Irwin finance implementation shows iTANZ using NetSuite as a connected financial core rather than an isolated ERP—preserving integration with the council systems that already run revenue and asset operations.
