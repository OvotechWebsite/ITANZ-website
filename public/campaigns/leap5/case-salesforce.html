Building a Connected Customer Experience Across Sales, Service and Digital Channels

Designed a Salesforce-based customer engagement architecture centred on a governed customer record.

The Problem

  • Customer information was distributed across CRM, service, marketing and core operational systems.
  • Sales and service teams were working with different versions of the customer record.
  • A growing volume of conversations had moved into informal channels such as WhatsApp.
  • Repeated manual data entry, limited customer-journey visibility and slow case resolution were creating operational friction.
  • The fragmented data foundation also limited readiness for AI-driven customer service.

The Solution

  • Unified customer profiles across sales and service.
  • Introduced structured case-management and automated routing workflows.
  • Brought WhatsApp Business communication into governed enterprise processes.
  • Integrated Salesforce with core operational and financial systems rather than replacing every platform.
  • Established customer-data quality and identity controls.
  • Enabled management dashboards for service and pipeline visibility.
  • Prepared the environment for governed AI-assisted summarisation and agent support.
Technologies Used

Salesforce Sales Cloud • Salesforce Service Cloud • Salesforce Data Cloud • MuleSoft • WhatsApp Business integration • Enterprise analytics • Governed AI / agent-assist capability

Impact We Created

One customer view

A more consistent customer view across teams.

Fewer handoffs

Reduced manual handoffs between sales, service and operations.

Traceable communication

Better traceability of customer communications.

Faster routing

Faster routing of enquiries to the correct teams.

Management visibility

Improved management visibility across customer-facing processes.

Ready for automation

A stronger data foundation for future automation and AI initiatives.

The Conclusion

Customer-experience transformation creates the most value when CRM, data, integration and governance are designed together. By connecting Salesforce to the wider enterprise environment, iTANZ can help organisations move from fragmented customer interactions toward a more unified, measurable and scalable customer operating model.
