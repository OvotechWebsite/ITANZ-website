CONNECT CITIZEN AND SERVICE PAYMENTS WITH ORACLE NETSUITE FINANCE

iTANZ integrates service-payment operations with Oracle NetSuite Finance to reduce manual journals and create an auditable path from the original payment to the general ledger.

HOW THE WORKFLOW RUNS BUSINESS OUTCOMES

Best fit for: Local government, councils, public-sector agencies and service organisations collecting fees across online, EFTPOS and counter channels.

BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Service fees may be collected across online portals, EFTPOS terminals and counter payments, while finance operates in a separate ledger. Without a controlled integration, teams may rely on manual journals and spreadsheet reconciliation, making it harder to trace revenue back to the originating service transaction.

HOW ITANZ AND ORACLE NETSUITE ADDRESS IT

Operational payment transactions are collected through the service platform and transferred through a scheduled batch into iTANZ Finance on Oracle NetSuite. Revenue is directed to the relevant income accounts, finance confirms that the batch agrees with the source-system total and users can drill back to the original payment detail.

HOW THE WORKFLOW RUNS

  1. 1

    Collect payments across service channels

    Capture online, EFTPOS and over-the-counter payments against the relevant application, permit, licence or service.

  2. 2

    Run the scheduled finance batch

    Push the day’s operational payment transactions into Oracle NetSuite Finance at the configured cut-off.

  3. 3

    Post to the correct income accounts

    Map each fee type to the appropriate general-ledger account during the automated posting process.

  4. 4

    Reconcile source totals to the GL batch

    Confirm that the operational receipts total matches the debit and credit values posted into Finance.

  5. 5

    Drill back to the original transaction

    Trace the GL entry to the applicant or customer, reference number, fee type, amount and payment method.

BUSINESS OUTCOMES

Automated revenue posting

Reduce manual journal creation by moving approved operational transactions into Finance through a scheduled integration.

Multi-channel visibility

Bring online, EFTPOS and counter receipts into one finance reconciliation process.

Daily control and exception review

Compare source totals with the GL batch and flag any variance for investigation.

End-to-end traceability

Drill from the finance entry back to the original service and payment detail.
