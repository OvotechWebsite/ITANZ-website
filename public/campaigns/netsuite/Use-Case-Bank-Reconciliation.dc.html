BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Daily bank reconciliation becomes slow and error-prone when finance teams manually compare every system transaction with every bank line. Consolidated bank payments, fees, direct credits and timing differences make the process even harder to control.

HOW ITANZ AND ORACLE NETSUITE ADDRESS IT

The finance team imports the prior-day bank statement into iTANZ Finance. Oracle NetSuite matches routine transactions using available reference information, locks cleared items and presents unmatched transactions in a dedicated exception worklist. Finance users can then resolve grouped payments, fees and other exceptions before final sign-off.

HOW THE WORKFLOW RUNS

  1. 1

    Import the bank statement

    Load the prior-day statement in the supported bank format into iTANZ Finance.

  2. 2

    Automatically match routine transactions

    Use amount, date and reference information to clear transactions that meet the configured matching rules.

  3. 3

    Surface genuine exceptions

    Present unmatched lines, bank fees, direct credits, timing differences and consolidated payments in a dedicated worklist.

  4. 4

    Resolve many-to-one and manual matches

    Allow the finance user to select multiple system transactions and reconcile them against one consolidated bank line, with value validation.

  5. 5

    Finalise and sign off

    Confirm that the cleared balance agrees with the bank statement closing balance and retain the reconciliation history for review.

BUSINESS OUTCOMES

Keep unresolved items visible in a controlled worklist rather than offline spreadsheets.

Support complex matches

Resolve consolidated payments and many-to-one scenarios with validated totals.

Audit-ready reconciliation

Record the user, date and matching method for completed reconciliation activity.
