BUSINESS CHALLENGE

When procurement and fixed-asset records are maintained separately, finance teams must re-enter supplier, cost and purchase information. This increases the risk of inconsistent records, delayed capitalisation, missed depreciation and difficult asset-to-GL reconciliation.

HOW ITANZ AND ORACLE NETSUITE ADDRESS IT

When an item is received and meets the configured capitalisation rules, the workflow can prompt direct conversion into Oracle NetSuite Fixed Assets. Purchase information carries into the asset record, finance completes the accounting setup, recurring depreciation posts to the GL and revaluation activity remains traceable.

HOW THE WORKFLOW RUNS

  1. 1

    Receive the qualifying purchase

    Confirm goods receipt in iTANZ Procurement using the original purchase-order record.

  2. 2

    Detect the capitalisation requirement

    Apply configured value and useful-life rules to identify purchases that should become fixed assets.

  3. 3

    Convert without duplicate entry

    Transfer supplier, cost, purchase date and description directly into the fixed-asset record.

  4. 4

    Complete asset setup and depreciation

    Assign the asset category, location, custodian, depreciation method, useful life and residual value, then include the asset in the recurring depreciation run.

  5. 5

    Post journals and maintain valuation history

    Post depreciation journals to the finance GL and retain the asset history required for review, revaluation and audit.

BUSINESS OUTCOMES

No duplicate data entry

Reuse approved procurement data instead of recreating the asset record manually.

Timely capitalisation

Prompt finance when a received item meets the configured asset criteria.

Automated recurring accounting

Process depreciation across active assets and post the resulting journals to the GL.

Stronger asset governance

Retain purchase, setup, depreciation and valuation history against the asset record.
